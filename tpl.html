About The Organisation

The Pro League

The Pro League (TPL) is Bangalore's premier sports and entertainment organisation — the city's definitive answer to what a sporting spectacle should look like.

We operate at the intersection of Entertainment, Competition, and Community. TPL exists to hand local talent a professional-grade platform while delivering an atmosphere that rivals a sold-out concert for every single spectator.

"TPL isn't just a league. It's a spectacle."

From Fit Fusion 2.0 at the Mall of Asia — which pulled over 2,000 attendees — to intimate street battles at Depot 18, we've built 15+ events that prove Bangalore's sports culture punches well above its weight.

Off the pitch, TPL leads the "Giving Back, The TPL Way" initiative — a community programme involving volunteering, local donations, and making sport accessible beyond the scoreboard.
