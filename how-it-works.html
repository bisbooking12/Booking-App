Common Questions
Frequently Asked
How long does it take to get my event listed? ▶
From initial enquiry to a live event page typically takes 3–7 business days, depending on how quickly we can align on design and approvals during the strategy call.
Do I need to move my ticketing to NEXUS? ▶
No — your ticketing, sign-ups, and audience comms remain entirely with you. NEXUS handles discovery and your custom page; attendees click through directly to wherever you manage tickets.
What does a custom event page include? ▶
A fully branded, bespoke event page built to your aesthetic — including event details, imagery, ticket links, organizer info, countdown timer, and SEO optimization. Designed in collaboration with you during the strategy call.
What analytics will I get access to? ▶
Your admin dashboard shows real-time page views, click-through rates, audience location data, device breakdowns, and engagement over time — so you always know how your listing is performing.
Can I list recurring or multiple events? ▶
Absolutely. We offer recurring event partnerships for organizers who run regular events. Reach out via the Partner With Us form to discuss a tailored arrangement.
Is there a cost to list my event? ▶
Pricing is discussed during the strategy call and depends on the scope of your listing, the scale of your event, and the type of partnership. Get in touch and we'll put together a proposal.
